# Contributing

Bug reports are the most useful contribution: a `.scad` file that converts wrong, or doesn't convert, attached to an
issue with what you expected.

Pull requests are welcome too. This repo is exported from Forgent3D's main repository, so an accepted change is
applied there and comes back here with the next export.

openscad-to-step is offered under the AGPL and, separately, under a commercial license. To keep that possible, by
submitting a contribution you agree that:

- you wrote it, or otherwise have the right to submit it;
- it is licensed to everyone under the AGPL-3.0, like the rest of this repo;
- Forgent3D may also distribute it under other license terms, including the commercial license.
